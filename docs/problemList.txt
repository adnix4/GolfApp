[x] D11. DESK STAFF CANNOT MARK A CASH FEE PAID (found 2026-07-31)
        fee-paid is [Authorize(Policy="OrgAdmin")] (TeamController.cs:199) while
        check-in is EventStaff (PlayerController.cs:91). An EventStaff user

        every reader treats as unpaid.
